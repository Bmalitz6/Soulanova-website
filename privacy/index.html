Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Soulanova offers a wellness website and free member experience. This notice describes how information is handled when you browse, register, use workouts, or contact us. For questions or privacy requests, email support@soulanova.com.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we receive your email address, optional profile name, and authentication-related account information. Our authentication provider manages passwords and sign-in credentials. We also store workout completion history, session durations, and the email-marketing preference you choose. If you contact us, we receive the content and contact details in your message.

Hosting and authentication services may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request logs, and session identifiers for operation and security.

How we use information

  • Create and secure your account; provide sign-in, verification, and password recovery.
  • Give you access to free classes, recipes, and articles, and save workout progress.
  • Answer support requests, diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and maintain site reliability.
  • Send optional wellness and launch marketing emails only when your recorded preference allows it.

Services that process information

We use Supabase for member authentication and account/workout data, Resend to deliver authentication emails, and Vercel to host the website. Each provider processes information needed to provide its service and may operate in different jurisdictions. Their own privacy notices describe their practices.

Marketing choices

Marketing emails are optional and are not required for a free membership. The signup checkbox starts unchecked. You can update your preference under My account. Promotional emails, when introduced, must include a working unsubscribe mechanism. Essential account and security emails may still be sent.

Cookies and storage

We use browser-based session and authentication storage needed to keep you signed in. Providers may use security-related cookies or similar technologies. If additional analytics or advertising trackers are introduced, this notice and any required consent choices should be updated before activation.

Retention and security

We retain information while needed to provide your membership, meet applicable obligations, resolve issues, and protect the service. Retention periods may vary by record type. We use access controls and service-provider security measures, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to receive marketing and request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information at support@soulanova.com. We may need to verify your identity and retain limited records where law or legitimate security needs require. Additional rights may apply based on your location.

Updates

We may update this policy as features and services evolve. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised effective date.

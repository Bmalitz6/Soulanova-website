## Proposal guardrails

- Beginner sequences use seated mobility and omit the Warrior, side-angle, chair-supported tree, and standing calf-stretch demonstrations.
- Intermediate sequences may include seated hip mobility, chair-supported Warrior I/II, and supported calf stretches. Side-angle and chair-supported tree remain reserved for Advanced in this proposal.
- Advanced sequences include the more involved chair-supported standing and balance demonstrations.
- When a workout includes a movement from a defined right/left family, the adjacent segments show Right then Left. The app validator rejects one-sided or reversed pairs.
- Each of the 36 sequences has 12 distinct movement IDs. The 36 source video files are referenced in place; no video or audio is copied, regenerated, or altered.
- All combinations in this matrix are owner-approved. Narration remains in production review and will be generated from the approved scripts; it remains disabled for members until recording review is complete.

## Playback

- Beginner repeats every movement twice; Intermediate three times; Advanced four times.
- Each completed movement except the twelfth is followed by a five-second black Up Next screen with the actual following movement name and thumbnail.
- Pause freezes a movement or the transition countdown. Resume continues it. Next Movement skips one segment.
- The selected 12-segment workout ends on its own completion screen. Playback never starts another workout or changes level automatically.

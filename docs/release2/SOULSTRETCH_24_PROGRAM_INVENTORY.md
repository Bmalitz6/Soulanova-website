# SoulStretch 24-program inventory

Candidate catalog: 18 level-based programs (six at each level) plus six short body-area routines. All sequences use only the 22 owner-approved movement videos. Routine estimates use the approved clip lengths, a 30-second setup allowance, and six seconds between movements; they are approximate.

Difficulty describes sequence length, balance demand, and coordination. It is not a medical assessment. Use nearby support and skip a movement if it feels unsteady or uncomfortable.

## Beginner

### Gentle Full-Body Reset — 6 min (9 movements)
Focus: Full body. A supported, unhurried introduction to shoulder, hip, and leg mobility.
Ordered movements: Overhead reach → Shoulder Stretch → Cross-body shoulder stretch, variation A → Staggered-stance hip-flexor stretch, variation A → Standing hamstring stretch, variation A → Standing calf stretch, variation A → Calf Raises → Toe Raises → Chest Stretch.

### Upper-Body Ease — 4 min (5 movements)
Focus: Upper body. Gentle shoulder, chest, and overhead movements. Stay in a comfortable range.
Ordered movements: Overhead shoulder stretch → Overhead reach → Cross-body shoulder stretch, variation A → Shoulder Stretch → Chest Stretch.

### Lower-Leg Foundations — 3 min (4 movements)
Focus: Calves & ankles. A short, supported sequence for calf flexibility and the lower legs, ending with gentle ankle movement.
Ordered movements: Standing calf stretch, variation A → Standing calf stretch, variation B → Calf Raises → Toe Raises.

### Supported Hips & Legs — 4 min (5 movements)
Focus: Hips & legs. A steady, supported combination of hip mobility and simple ankle movement. Keep a chair or wall within reach and move without rushing.
Ordered movements: Staggered-stance hip-flexor stretch, variation B → Hip Rotations → Standing knee-to-chest stretch → Calf Raises → Toe Raises.

### Gentle Standing Balance — 3 min (4 movements)
Focus: Balance & legs. A steady introduction to supported single-leg positions and simple lower-leg movements. Use a sturdy support throughout.
Ordered movements: Standing knee-to-chest stretch → Standing figure-four stretch, variation A → Calf Raises → Toe Raises.

### Easy Chest & Shoulders — 3 min (3 movements)
Focus: Shoulders & chest. An unhurried upper-body sequence through chest opening and comfortable shoulder mobility.
Ordered movements: Chest Stretch → Cross-body shoulder stretch, variation B → Cross-body shoulder stretch, variation A.

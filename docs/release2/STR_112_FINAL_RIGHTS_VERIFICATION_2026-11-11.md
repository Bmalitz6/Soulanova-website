## Exactly what is missing and how to resolve it

1. **Generation/input linkage:** an existing job/export/project record or authoritative production record identifying this exact output and its actual motion video, source photograph and character-reference inputs. Identify each by path/hash. An Act-Two filename alone is not that record.
2. **Input rights:** ownership/provenance or a licence covering the actual input images/recordings and intended commercial website use, redistribution and AI/derivative processing as applicable. Identify the creator/licensor and the scope of permission; do not substitute a product approval checklist.
3. **Likeness/participant rights as applicable:** the relevant person's release/permission for actual human inputs/performance, or documented wholly synthetic creation and source-reference provenance if no real person's likeness was used. Do not invent a release for a fictional person, and do not infer a fictional identity from how an image looks.
4. **Provider record where applicable:** establish which tools/accounts were actually used and any applicable generation/export entitlement at that time. Provider permission cannot cure missing rights to the inputs.

The owner can supply the existing records or their local paths. If the owner created and owns the inputs, a dated, asset-specific provenance/rights declaration can identify that basis and the actual inputs, with underlying third-party permissions where needed. It must establish facts, not merely repeat “approved video.” Redact unrelated billing/personal information; never provide credentials or tokens. If no adequate rights basis can be established, keep the substitutions blocked and obtain a properly licensed alternative under a separate reviewed mapping decision.

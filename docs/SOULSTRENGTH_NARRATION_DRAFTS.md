# SoulStrength recording drafts — not approved for recording yet

These are first-pass text cues for later human review. No voice was synthesized and no audio was generated. They intentionally avoid weight prescriptions. Use a comfortable load and range; stop if the movement causes pain. Technique wording needs instructor review before recording.

| Movement | Draft voice cue |
| --- | --- |
| Dumbbell Floor Press | Lie comfortably with knees bent and feet supported. Lower your elbows with control, then breathe out as you press the weights upward without locking your elbows. |
| Dumbbell Bench Press | Set your feet firmly and keep your shoulders relaxed. Lower the weights toward a comfortable chest-height range, then breathe out as you press smoothly upward. |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | Settle your back against the incline and keep your feet steady. Lower the weights slowly, then press upward while keeping the shoulders soft. |
| Dumbbell Chest Fly | Keep a gentle bend in your elbows and your shoulders relaxed. Open only as far as feels comfortable, then bring your arms together without forcing the range. |
| Dumbbell Pullover | Keep your ribs relaxed and hold the weight securely with both hands. Move behind your head only within a comfortable range, then return slowly above your chest. |
| One-Arm Dumbbell Row | Support yourself with your free hand and keep your back long. Draw the weight toward your hip, then lower it slowly; repeat on both sides. |
| Bent-Over Dumbbell Row | Hinge gently at the hips with a long back and soft knees. Pull your elbows toward your sides, then lower the weights without swinging. |
| Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | Rest your chest comfortably on the support and let your arms hang easily. Draw your elbows back, then lower the weights slowly. |
| Reverse Fly | Hinge slightly with a long back and relaxed neck. Open your arms only to a comfortable height, then lower them with control. |
| Renegade Row | Begin in a stable, supported plank or from your knees with feet comfortably wide. Row one weight toward your side without twisting, then switch sides; use a supported row if balance feels difficult. |
| Seated Dumbbell Overhead Press | Sit tall with your feet grounded and weights near shoulder height. Breathe out as you press only as high as feels comfortable, then lower slowly. |
| Arnold Press | Sit or stand steadily with a light, comfortable grip. Rotate smoothly as you press, keeping the motion controlled and pain-free. |
| Lateral Raise | Keep a soft bend in your elbows and shoulders relaxed. Lift the weights out to a comfortable height, then lower them slowly. |
| Dumbbell Shrug | Stand tall and let your arms rest by your sides. Lift your shoulders gently toward your ears, then release them slowly without rolling. |
| Alternating Curl | Keep your elbows near your sides and wrists straight. Curl one weight without swinging, lower slowly, and alternate arms. |
| Hammer Curl | Keep your palms facing inward and shoulders relaxed. Bend your elbows to lift, then lower the weights with control. |
| Concentration Curl | Sit with your elbow supported against your inner thigh. Curl within a comfortable range, lower slowly, and repeat on the other arm. |
| Zottman Curl | Keep your elbows close as you curl with palms turning upward. Rotate gently at the top, then lower with palms facing down and control the whole motion. |
| Overhead Triceps Extension | Hold one weight securely and keep your upper arms comfortable beside your head. Bend your elbows within a pain-free range, then extend gently without locking. |
| Triceps Kickback | Support your body with a long, steady back and elbow near your side. Extend your forearm behind you, then return slowly without swinging. |
| Lying Dumbbell Triceps Extension | Lie comfortably and keep your upper arms steady. Bend your elbows to lower the weights beside your head, then extend without locking. |
| Close-Grip Dumbbell Floor Press | Lie with knees bent and weights above your chest, held comfortably close. Lower your upper arms to the floor gently, then press upward with control. |
| Goblet Squat | Hold one weight close to your chest and stand with feet comfortably apart. Sit your hips back within a comfortable depth, then press through your feet to stand. |
| Sumo Squat | Set your feet wider with toes turned out only as comfortable. Bend your knees over your toes, then stand smoothly without forcing depth. |
| Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | Keep a soft bend in your knees and weights close to your legs. Hinge at the hips while your back stays long, then stand by bringing your hips forward gently. |
| Reverse Lunge | Hold a stable stance and step one foot back only as far as comfortable. Lower gently with support nearby if needed, return to center, and switch sides. |
| Split Squat | Keep your feet staggered and hold support if balance helps. Lower in a small comfortable range, rise smoothly, then repeat on the other side. |
| Dumbbell Step-Up | Choose a low, steady step and keep a hand near support. Step up with control, stand tall, and lower slowly; practice both sides. |
| Standing Calf Raise | Stand near a sturdy support with feet grounded. Rise through the balls of your feet, pause briefly, then lower slowly. |
| Dumbbell Glute Bridge | Lie comfortably with knees bent and feet grounded. Breathe out as you lift your hips gently, then lower without arching your back. |
| Weighted Dead Bug | Lie on your back and keep your breathing easy. Move one arm and opposite leg only as far as you can keep your trunk comfortable, then switch sides. |
| Dumbbell Russian Twist | Sit with feet grounded and hold a light weight close to your body. Turn your rib cage gently from side to side without forcing the rotation. |
| Dumbbell Side Bend | Stand tall with a light weight at one side and shoulders relaxed. Slide your hand down only a little, return to center, and switch sides without leaning forward. |
| Suitcase March | Hold one comfortable weight at your side and stand near support. March slowly without leaning, then switch hands and repeat. |
| Dumbbell Thruster | Use a light weight and a comfortable squat depth. Stand smoothly as you press, exhaling through the effort; reduce the range or separate the squat and press if needed. |
| Dumbbell Clean to Press | Start with light weights and keep them close as you bring them to shoulder height. Press only within a comfortable range, then lower slowly; practice one side at a time if needed. |
| Farmer Carry | Hold comfortable weights by your sides and stand tall. Walk slowly with relaxed shoulders and steady breaths, turning carefully and setting the weights down safely. |
| Dumbbell Snatch | Use a very light weight and practice the lift slowly from a stable stance. Guide the weight overhead only if comfortable; otherwise use a supported one-arm row or deadlift. |

## Recording brief

- Target one concise 10–18 second cue per movement, with enough quiet around the words for clean editing.
- Keep the approved coach voice and Soulanova pronunciation standard; do not add another class welcome.
- Record both sides where the cue requests it, but avoid repeating a full introduction between sides.
- Capture a separate short warm-up intro and consistent rest/recovery cue only if existing approved narration cannot serve.
- Do not record these until the original user-supplied exercise list is found and the movement names are approved.

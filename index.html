LUNCH · 15 min

Lemony Chickpea & Cucumber Bowl

View recipe +

Ingredients

1 can chickpeas, rinsed; 1 cucumber; 1 cup cherry tomatoes; 2 tbsp olive oil; 1 lemon; 2 tbsp parsley

Directions

Drain chickpeas and chop vegetables. Toss with olive oil, lemon juice and parsley. Season lightly and serve.

BREAKFAST · 10 min + chilling

Berry Walnut Overnight Oats

View recipe +

Ingredients

1 cup rolled oats; 1 cup milk or fortified alternative; ½ cup plain yogurt; 1 cup berries; ¼ cup walnuts

Directions

Mix oats, milk and yogurt. Refrigerate overnight. Divide into bowls and top with berries and walnuts.

DINNER · 30 min

Herb-Baked Salmon & Vegetables

View recipe +

Ingredients

2 salmon fillets; 1 zucchini; 1 bell pepper; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 lemon; dill or parsley

Directions

Heat oven to 400°F. Place fish and chopped vegetables on a baking tray. Drizzle with oil and lemon; bake until salmon reaches 145°F.

01 / HOW IT WORKS

A flexible way
to nourish yourself.

Mediterranean-style eating focuses on overall habits rather than rigid rules. Fill your meals with plants, use olive oil, and enjoy a variety of nourishing foods.

❧

Start with plants

Vegetables, fruit, beans, lentils, nuts and whole grains are everyday staples.

◒

Choose healthy fats

Use extra-virgin olive oil and enjoy nuts and seeds.

≈

Include fish

Enjoy fish regularly, alongside beans and other protein choices.

♡

Make it enjoyable

Cook when you can and share satisfying meals with others.

02 / FOODS TO ENJOY

Colorful food.
Simple choices.

Think of the Mediterranean diet as a flexible pattern, not a list of forbidden foods.

Enjoy often

  • Vegetables and fruit
  • Beans and lentils
  • Whole grains
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Nuts and seeds

Include regularly

  • Fish and seafood
  • Yogurt and natural cheeses
  • Eggs and poultry
  • Fresh herbs and spices

Limit where possible

  • Sugary drinks and sweets
  • Refined grains
  • Processed meats
  • Highly processed foods

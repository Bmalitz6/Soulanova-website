About FoodScore & source data

Each category has its own profile. Solid foods, meals and snacks use sugars, saturated fat, sodium, fiber and protein. Drinks use total sugars, sodium, saturated fat and energy per 100 ml. Oils use reported unsaturated and saturated fat ratios. A category or nutrient that cannot be established is never guessed.

Ingredient points require a complete recognized set of declared percentages. Missing ingredient or processing points are excluded. At least 75 weighted points and all category nutrient inputs must be available: score = round(100 × earned points ÷ assessed points). The interface shows coverage and every scored factor; coverage measures completeness, not accuracy. Incomplete scores are not comparable to scores with different coverage.

85–100 Excellent · 70–84 Good · 50–69 Moderate · 30–49 Limit frequency · 0–29 Occasional. These labels and numerical cutoffs are provisional design choices, not validated frequency advice. Check portions and the package label; no score establishes allergen safety or suitability for you.

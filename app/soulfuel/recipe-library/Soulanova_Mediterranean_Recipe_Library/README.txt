SOULANOVA — Mediterranean Recipe Library
========================================
Four collections: Breakfast (23), Lunch (22), Dinner (21), Snacks (9)
Total recipes: 75

TO OPEN ON YOUR MAC
1. Unzip the downloaded archive.
2. Open index.html in Safari, Chrome, or your preferred browser.
3. Choose a recipe category.
4. Find a recipe, or search by name or ingredient.
5. Click PRINT RECIPE on that recipe. The print dialog prints only the chosen recipe, not the entire category.

FILES
- index.html — category landing page
- breakfast.html — 23 recipes
- lunch.html — 22 recipes
- dinner.html — 21 recipes
- snacks.html — 9 recipes
- recipes.json — structured recipe content for later integration into the Soulanova app

All HTML files work offline and require no server, account, or external libraries.
The navigation assumes the HTML files stay in the same folder.

EDITORIAL AND SOURCE NOTES
- The introductions were rewritten in Soulanova's gentle, inviting voice.
- The ingredients and preparation directions were preserved from the uploaded Pages files,
  except for cleaning a copied sign-in overlay and correcting split recipe text.
- Nutrition values come from the supplied documents; no missing values were calculated.
- 'Really Green Smoothie' did not contain a nutrition panel. The page states this.
- One 'Serving Size 1 sandwich' nutrition panel was placed under 'Veggie Wraps' in the
  supplied Lunch Pages file. It was provisionally associated with 'The Only High-Protein
  Veggie Sandwich Formula You Need' and flagged visibly for verification.
- One granola recipe included an OCR/source layout mix-up: serving ingredients were
  interspersed with the preparation steps. The supplied text was reorganized.
- Recipes sourced from other publishers should be reviewed for permission before
  posting publicly, especially where their original instructions are reproduced.
